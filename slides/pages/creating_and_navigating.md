# Creating and navigating revisions

---
title: Common commands
level: 2
---

<v-clicks>

- **`jj log`** shows revision history
- **`jj undo`** undoes an operation (think “repository undo”, powered by the operation log)
- **`jj new`** starts a new, empty revision on top of a parent revision
- **`jj describe` / `jj desc`** updates the change description (commit message)
- **`jj edit`** makes an existing revision the working-copy revision

</v-clicks>
